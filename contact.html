Questions before booking

  • Who owns the rights to the final images?

    You do. Once a project is delivered and paid, you receive full commercial usage rights to every final image, across web, print, packaging, social and paid campaigns, with no recurring licence fees.

  • What file formats and resolutions are delivered?

    Every image arrives as a high-resolution PNG and JPG, sized for print and for screen. We can also supply cropped variants for social, marketplace and banner placements on request.

  • How long does a project take?

    AI-generated campaign imagery is delivered in 5 to 7 business days from the moment your brief and references are confirmed. Larger seasonal packages are scheduled together at booking.

  • How many revisions are included?

    Two revision rounds are included in every package. Each round covers styling, lighting, pose and background adjustments, so the final images match your direction.

  • Do you need physical product samples or photos of them?

    Clear photographs of your products are usually enough. If a piece has complex texture, reflection or fine detail, a physical sample helps us reproduce it faithfully.

  • Which industries do you work with?

    We are serving fashion, beauty, jewelry, food, hospitality and lifestyle brands worldwide, adapting the visual language to each category and audience.

  • Can one model look be reused across a season?

    Yes. Unlimited AI model variations are available within a booked package, so one consistent look can carry every drop, colourway and channel through the season.

Art direction is the craft

AI is a production tool, and art direction is the craft. We began as a small art-direction practice. Every frame is storyboarded before generation, and lighting and styling references are chosen like a shoot. A person retouches each pass by hand. We use no stock-looking faces, and each brand keeps its own consistent model casting.

No photoshoots, no physical models, no studio rental.
