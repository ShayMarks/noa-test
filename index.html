Imagery without the shoot

FRAMELESS LAB builds realistic AI models and campaign visuals for fashion, beauty, jewelry, food and hospitality brands worldwide. No photoshoots, no physical models, no studio rental. Your finished images arrive in 5 to 7 business days, with two revision rounds included in every package.

Our services

  • Fashion and swimwear lookbooks

    Full seasonal lookbooks without a single shoot day. Realistic AI models wear your garments in the settings, poses and light your art direction calls for, with unlimited model variations inside a booked package, so every drop feels consistent and ready for launch.

  • Beauty and skincare campaigns

    Close, luminous imagery for serums, palettes and fragrance. We shape skin texture, light and mood to match your brand, then deliver campaign sets in five to seven business days, with two revision rounds included so the final look is exactly yours.

  • Jewelry imagery

    Rings, chains and watches shown on convincing models with crisp detail on every facet and clasp. Editorial close-ups and lifestyle frames come from one product set, starting at $1,200, without studio rental or physical models.

  • Food and beverage visuals

    Appetizing hero shots for menus, packaging and social feeds. Steam, condensation, texture and styling are composed to your brief, giving you premium visuals for every product without a kitchen, a stylist or a production day.

  • Hospitality content

    Hotels, restaurants and venues pictured full of life: guests, rooms, tables and terraces in warm, believable light. Remote collaboration across every time zone keeps your launch calendar moving and your imagery consistent.

  • Lifestyle and custom production

    Campaign packages from $4,500 with up to 40 final images, built around your story. From advertising concepts to bespoke commercial scenes, we produce what a traditional shoot would, without the cost or complexity.
